The story of the Paris Inn

Before the location became the Paris Inn, it was the Paris Surf Shop. Founded by Chip Foster, the co-founer of Chip and Pepper Foster clothing. It's style features rustic elements, such as a sliding barn door at the entrance and an axe as the door handle. We sell oven-baked pizzas and flatbreads, as well as many other delicious foods and drinks.
