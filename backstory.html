Paris Inn Pizza Co.

The story of the Paris Inn


Built in the 1830's, the building that the Paris Inn Pizza Co. calls home was originally a functioning inn. By the 70's it had transitioned to a bar, and it had a very large role to play in local entertainment. As one user on Facebook quotes,

"The Paris Inn was the most happening bar in the 70s and 80s, a lot of good bands were there. Then over to the Royal for last call!"

After that, in the 90's, it became a bar known as "The Trough." Then it became the Paris Surf Shop in 2017, before becoming Paris Inn Pizza Co, founded by Chip Foster, the co-founder of Chip and Pepper Foster clothing.


It's style now features rustic elements that tie it back to it's roots, such as a sliding barn door at the entrance and an axe as the door handle. If you look up at the roof in the back of the restaurant, by the kitchen, you will notice that the rafters in the roof have been repaired over the years by simply fastening another piece of wood to the side, creating a detail that I find quite interesting. The Paris Inn Pizza Co. sells oven-baked pizzas and flatbreads, stone fired on their own pizza oven, as well as many other delicious foods and drinks.
